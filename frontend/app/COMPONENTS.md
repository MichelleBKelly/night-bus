# Components

- screen: Mobile page column. Used on sign in, both homes, the trip sheet, repairs, members, and the builder screen list.
- topbar: The page title. Used on every screen. The title is the trip name on a home, or the task name on sign in, the sheet, repairs, and members.
- card: Bordered group with a heading and a list or a short note. Used for the name picker, who is coming, drop-off, the run, stops, the record, other trips, bus totals, repairs, and members.
- btn-primary: The single main action, in the accent colour, placed directly under the decision it belongs to. Used once on every screen.
- btn-secondary: The lesser action under the primary: Sign out, Today, or Trips.
- field: Label plus a text input. Used for the PIN, the trip readings, fuel, hours, a repair, a member, and an "Other reason". The home address also opens a suggestion list for Kitchener–Waterloo and nearby.
- list-row: A full-width row. The label is bold. The value is regular, unless it is a tag. A pressed row is the selected name or a member who is aboard. When a row also shows an address, the address goes on its own line under the name ("Gets off at…"), never beside it. Used for people, members, stops, totals, and the builder list.
- spot-edit: Opens in place of a member's row to choose where they get off: Office, Home, Usual spot (if set), or Another address with an address field, then Done. Used in "Set up this trip" and on the driver's finish step.
- check-row: A list-row with a native checkbox or radio button first. Checkboxes pick who is coming. Radio buttons pick one of: the trip's driver, Volunteer or Paid, where a member gets off, the drop-off reason, and wheelchair Yes or No. The optional reason in "Set up this trip" is the only group where tapping the ticked row again un-ticks it.
- tag: A small boxed label on a row, such as a role, Aboard, or Sign in. Not used for trip status.
- status-tag: Plain bold text at the top left of the driver home, such as "Assigned to you". Trip screens show status as a Status row in the details instead.
- counter: A large figure. Used for kilometres and pay on a sent trip and while the end of a trip is being entered.
- banner: A status or instruction strip. Used for the trip state, form errors, the Members note, and the builder-list note.
- table: Unused. Kept for the week grid if reporting grows into columns.
- summary-box: A gray totals box. Used for the sent-trip result and the live kilometres and pay.
